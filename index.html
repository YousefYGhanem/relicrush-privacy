Relic Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Relic Rush ("the game") is developed by Yousef Ghanem. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

The game never asks for your real name, email or location. Signing in (see "Online features" below) is optional; without it the game works fully offline.

Your progress (coins, gems, purchased items, challenges, best scores and tutorial status) is saved on your device, in a file the game creates in its private storage. If you sign in, a backup copy is also kept online (see below).

Optional gameplay statistics

After your first run the game asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay statistics. Nothing is collected unless you choose "Yes, share", and you can turn it off at any time in Settings ("Share stats").

If you agree, the game sends usage data to Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies, to help balance the game. This includes:

  • Gameplay events: run distance, coins and gems collected, what ended the run, the world you reached, how long the run lasted, whether you used a save, which runner you played, finishing the tutorial, and items bought in the in-game shop.
  • Technical data Unity collects automatically: a random installation identifier created by Unity (not linked to your name or Google account), device model, operating system version, game version, language, and approximate country derived from your connection.

This data is used only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Unity processes it on our behalf under the Unity Privacy Policy. Turning "Share stats" off stops collection and sends Unity a request to delete the data collected from your device.

Online features (Google Play Games sign-in)

The game can sign you in with your Google Play Games account. When signed in, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) to:

  • identify your game account: Unity receives a player identifier linked to your Play Games account (not your email or Google password);
  • back up your game progress so you can restore it on another device;
  • show a leaderboard: the username you choose for the game and your best score are visible to other players. Choose a username that does not reveal your real name.

This data is used only to run these features. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Unity processes it on our behalf under the Unity Privacy Policy; Google Play Games is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Network and third parties

Apart from the optional statistics and online features above, the game does not send data to any server, and it does not include advertising SDKs.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files. Tilt controls use the device's motion sensors, which the game reads only while you play and does not store or transmit.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information such as names, emails or precise location from anyone.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are added in a future version, purchases will be processed by Google Play, and this policy will be updated to say so before they are released.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes its saved progress from your device. You can also clear it from Android settings under Apps, Relic Rush, Storage. To delete gameplay statistics, turn off "Share stats" in Settings. To delete your online backup, username and leaderboard entry, email us from the game's feedback button (or the address below) with your username, and we will delete them within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
