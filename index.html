Relic Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Relic Rush ("the game") is developed by Yousef Ghanem. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

The game does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no online profile.

Your progress (coins, gems, purchased upgrades, best scores, and tutorial status) is saved only on your device, in a file the game creates in its private storage. This data never leaves your device.

Network and third parties

The game does not send data to any server, and it does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Nothing about your play is shared with anyone.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files. Tilt controls use the device's motion sensors, which the game reads only while you play and does not store or transmit.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are added in a future version, purchases will be processed by Google Play, and this policy will be updated to say so before they are released.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes its saved progress from your device. You can also clear it from Android settings under Apps, Relic Rush, Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
